Idea 1 / Multiplicity

Many conversations at once, on a single canvas.

Each chat is a window you can drag. The connections stay visible — cords from every conversation back to its origin in the cluster.

Idea 2 / Branching

Branch any conversation with one click.

When Claude marks a concept with [[brackets]], click it to spawn a new branch without losing the original. Or select any phrase and branch from there.

Idea 3 / Synthesis

Distill a whole cluster into one conclusion.

When a cluster matures, distill it into a synthesis — an exportable document that captures everything that happened inside.
